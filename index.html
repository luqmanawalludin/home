About

Creative with a purpose

I’m Luqman Awalludin, a singer, music producer, educator, photographer, and videographer. My work is shaped by the belief that creativity is more than expression.

Through my music, I share emotion and authenticity. Through my visual work, I capture stories and atmosphere. Through my teaching, I inspire others to grow, create, and believe in their own voice.
